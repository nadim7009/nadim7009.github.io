Projects

IrrigaSmart: 12-hour soil-moisture risk predictionPython, scikit-learn
github.com/nadim7009/irrigasmart-soil-moisture-risk
  • Built an end-to-end pipeline on 31,492 sensor observations from vegetable farms in Belgium: removed duplicate and invalid readings, rebuilt crop-cycle boundaries and engineered 70 features from soil moisture, weather and field data.
  • Split the data by whole crop cycle so no field-season appeared in both training and test sets, then compared five models against a simple threshold rule.
  • HistGradientBoosting reached F1 0.977 and ROC-AUC 0.999 on unseen crop cycles and missed 44 at-risk cases against 81 for the rule baseline.
  • Checked calibration (Brier score 0.0038) and corrupted up to 30% of sensor readings in stress tests; F1 dropped by less than 0.003.
Robust plant disease classification with corruption-aware trainingPyTorch
  • Fine-tuned EfficientNetV2-S, ResNet50, DenseNet121 and EfficientNet-B0 on PlantVillage (54,305 images, 38 classes) with a group-aware split that keeps photos of the same leaf together.
  • Tested every model on nine corrupted copies of the test set (noise, low brightness and blur at three severity levels) and measured how far accuracy falls.
  • Trained ResNet50 with blur, noise and brightness corruptions and compared it with standard training on both clean and corrupted images.
  • Used Grad-CAM to check that predictions came from lesion areas and reported calibration with ECE and Brier score.
Microplastic segmentation in SEM imagesPyTorch
  • Trained a U-Net and a four-gate Attention U-Net on 237 labelled scanning electron microscopy images with BCE and Dice loss.
  • Compared the two models on Dice and IoU, removed each attention gate in turn in an ablation, and tested the models under noise, brightness and contrast changes.
Explainable carbon-footprint predictionXGBoost, SHAP
  • Compared Random Forest, XGBoost and an MLP on 18,000 records with 49 behavioural features, using a scikit-learn pipeline that fits imputation, outlier clipping and encoding on training folds only.
  • XGBoost gave the best test results of the three; SHAP values showed which lifestyle and digital-use features drove each prediction.
Undergraduate thesis: causal uplift modelling for email marketingCausal ML
