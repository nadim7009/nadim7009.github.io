Research interests

I study how deep learning models behave when real data stops looking like the benchmark, and how to explain what they learn. I am applying for an MSc to continue this work, with a focus on reliable computer vision for agriculture and the environment.

  • Robustness of computer vision models under distribution shift and image corruption (blur, noise, low light), and training methods that close the gap.
  • Explainable and trustworthy AI with Grad-CAM, SHAP and probability calibration, to check what a model looks at and whether its confidence holds up.
  • Segmentation of microscopy and environmental images with attention-based U-Net models.
  • Machine learning for agriculture and the environment: plant disease screening, soil-moisture forecasting, microplastic analysis and carbon-footprint modelling.
  • Causal machine learning, especially uplift modelling, to separate what a model predicts from what an action causes.

Research experience

Robustness of plant disease classifiers under image corruption2026
First author, paper [1]. Idea, all code and first manuscript draft
  • Designed a controlled benchmark: Gaussian noise, brightness reduction and Gaussian blur at three severity levels each, applied to the PlantVillage test set (54,305 images, 38 classes).
  • Built a group-aware split that keeps all photos of one leaf on the same side, to stop near-duplicate images from inflating validation scores.
  • Measured how much accuracy a strong EfficientNetV2-S baseline loses under each corruption and severity.
  • Tested corruption-aware training of ResNet50 with each corruption alone and all three together, tracking clean, mean corrupted and worst-case accuracy.
  • Compared four architectures and checked model reliability with Grad-CAM, reliability diagrams, expected calibration error and multiclass Brier score.
Attention-gated segmentation of microplastics in SEM images2026
Second author, paper [2]. Proposed the idea and wrote all of the code
  • Trained a baseline U-Net and a four-gate Attention U-Net on 237 labelled SEM images with a fixed group-aware split (167 / 36 / 34).
  • Compared the models on Dice, IoU, precision and recall to see where attention gates help and where they cost recall.
  • Ran an ablation that removes each attention gate in turn, and robustness tests under noise and reduced brightness and contrast.

Daffodil International University

IrrigaSmart: soil-moisture risk prediction from field sensors2026
Independent project, github.com/nadim7009/irrigasmart-soil-moisture-risk
